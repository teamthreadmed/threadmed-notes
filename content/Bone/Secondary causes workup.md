# Secondary causes workup
**Baseline:** CBC; CMP (calcium, albumin, phosphate, creatinine/eGFR, ALP, LFTs); 25-OH vitamin D; PTH; TSH; 24-h urine calcium and creatinine.

**Directed:**
- SPEP/serum free light chains: vertebral fracture, anemia, or unexplained low BMD
- tTG-IgA: iron deficiency, low urine calcium, GI symptoms
- Total testosterone (men); estradiol/FSH if menopausal status unclear
- Late-night salivary cortisol or 1-mg DST if cushingoid or fractures out of proportion
- Tryptase if mastocytosis features
- VFA or spine radiographs for height loss ≥ 4 cm or kyphosis
