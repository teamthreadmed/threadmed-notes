# Risk stratification
**Treat** (BHOF/AACE): hip or vertebral fragility fracture; T-score ≤ −2.5 at spine, femoral neck, total hip or 1/3 radius; or T-score −1.0 to −2.5 with FRAX 10-y MOF ≥ 20% or hip ≥ 3%.

**Very high risk** (AACE 2020), favors an anabolic first:
- fracture within the past 12 months, fracture on therapy, multiple fractures
- fracture on bone-harming drugs (e.g., glucocorticoids)
- T-score < −3.0, high fall risk
- FRAX MOF > 30% or hip > 4.5%

Next layer: [[Anabolics]] for very high risk, [[Bisphosphonates]] or [[Denosumab class]] for high risk.
