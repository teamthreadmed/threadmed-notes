## The stages
- **Reproductive years:** regular cycles. Fertility starts declining in the mid-30s, before cycles change
- **Perimenopause:** usually begins in the mid-to-late 40s and lasts about 4 to 8 years. Cycles first vary by 7 days or more, then periods are skipped. Symptoms often start here
- **Menopause:** the final menstrual period, confirmed only in hindsight after **12 months without a period**. Median age in the US is about 51
- **Postmenopause:** everything after. Hot flashes continue for years in many women; bone loss and genitourinary symptoms come to the fore

## What estrogen actually does
The common picture of a smooth decline is wrong. In perimenopause estrogen **swings**, sometimes higher than in the reproductive years, before it falls after the final period. This is why symptoms come and go, and why a single blood test is usually not useful to diagnose menopause after 45.

## Worth knowing
- Hot flashes last a median of about **7 years** overall, and longer when they start early in perimenopause
- Menopause before 40 (primary ovarian insufficiency) or before 45 (early menopause) needs evaluation and usually hormone replacement until the average age of menopause
- Contraception is still needed until menopause is confirmed
