## How to read it
Plot the patient's PTH against a **simultaneous** albumin-corrected or ionized calcium. The key move is recognising that a PTH inside the reference range is *inappropriate* when calcium is high (points to parathyroid-dependent disease) or low (points to hypoparathyroidism).

## Before you place the point
- Same-draw calcium, albumin, phosphate, magnesium, creatinine/eGFR
- 25-OH vitamin D
- Medications: thiazides, lithium, loop diuretics, antiresorptives, biotin (assay interference)
