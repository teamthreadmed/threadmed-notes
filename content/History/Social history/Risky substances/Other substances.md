Opioids, anabolic steroids, stimulants and other drugs.

**Capture:** Checklist with dates. **Values:** Opioids (chronic); anabolic-androgenic steroids / SARMs; stimulants; other

- Chronic opioids: hypogonadotropic hypogonadism; opioid-induced adrenal insufficiency.
- Anabolic steroids / SARMs: suppressed LH/FSH with hypogonadism after cessation; erythrocytosis; dyslipidemia (low HDL).
