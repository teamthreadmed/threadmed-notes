Systemic glucocorticoid exposure, current or past.

**Capture:** Structured: agent, dose, duration. **Values:** Prednisolone-equivalent mg/day; months of use; current (yes/no)

**FRAX definition:** oral glucocorticoids currently, or for more than 3 months, at a dose of prednisolone 5 mg/day or more (or equivalent).

Also feeds adrenal-suppression risk (dose, duration, recency).
